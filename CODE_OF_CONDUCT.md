# Code of conduct

Sol is built by people giving their time. To keep it worth giving:

- **Be kind and assume good faith.** Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- **Be specific.** "This breaks when the peer is offline, here is the log" helps. "This is
  garbage" doesn't.
- **No harassment**, no personal attacks, no sexualised or discriminatory remarks, in issues,
  pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives.
- **Beginners are welcome.** Nobody is born knowing what CGNAT is.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and block people who keep ignoring this. To report a
problem, write to the maintainer privately through the contact on their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/apv022).
